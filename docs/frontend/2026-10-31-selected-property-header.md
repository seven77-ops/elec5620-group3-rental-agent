# Selected property in the chat criteria area — 31 October 2026

The chat header now combines current housing filters, the selected property address and Contact property agent in one area. On wider screens the selected property and contact action sit to the right of the criteria; narrow screens wrap within the same area. Selection remains accessible when the current conversation has no filter context or filters have not loaded.

The compact chat banner no longer offers Generate lease draft or Review an existing contract. Contract assistant in the sidebar retains the drafting and upload/review functions. The full selected-property panel on the recommendations page retains its existing actions. The contact drawer, selection state and version warnings are preserved.

The implementation composes CandidateFollowup into RecommendationControls rather than creating another selected-property state. Existing filter edits, loading/retry behaviour and the contact drawer share their original logic. No business records or backend workflows were changed.
