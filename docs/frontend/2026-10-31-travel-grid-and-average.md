# Compact travel modes and catalogue average — 31 October 2026

The user asked for equal-sized travel-mode references instead of a large public-transport itinerary, then explicitly selected a catalogue average with expandable contributing modes. This replaces the earlier single-mode-only catalogue presentation; it does not change route matching.

Property details show Public transport, Driving, Cycling and eligible Walking in one compact grid. The public transport card uses the chosen valid itinerary or its estimated range. Complex legs and alternatives are under a collapsed Public transport route details disclosure. Visible itinerary titles use Recommended route / Alternative route rather than a long chain of modes. Recorded service, stop, transfer and timing facts remain available when expanded.

The Housing recommendations table labels its column Avg. to USYD. Each available displayed category counts once; public transport is one category, rather than separate train and bus entries. For exact references the arithmetic mean is rounded to the nearest minute. If a participating value is an estimate range, the lower and upper bounds are averaged separately and rounded outward. Missing references do not count as zero. The established rule omitting walking above 30 minutes remains, except when walking is the selected mode; this matches the categories in property details. Expanding the mode count shows the individual values and states that filtering uses the selected mode.

For example, public transport 50 minutes, driving 14 minutes and cycling 42 minutes produce Avg. ~35 min. The public transport reference remains 50 minutes for a public-transport filter. The display helper does not alter profiles, request mode, commute ceilings, transfer restrictions or backend routing.
