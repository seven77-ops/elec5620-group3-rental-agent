# Rental assistant user guide

This guide explains the reviewed rental assistant interface. The guide was checked against the implemented product on 31 October 2026. The interface and generated explanations use English. Original document quotations and supplied names retain their original text. Available AI assistance depends on the configured services.

This is product help. For tenancy rights, obligations and legal deadlines, use the separately cited official guidance and check its source. A property advertisement, an estimate, a saved choice and an unsigned draft each describe different stages of your search.

## Start a case and chat

Choose “New case” (New case) to start a rental workspace. Demonstration configurations may label this “New demo case”. A case holds the rental requirements, selected property, contract work and aftercare records that you save in it.

Open “AI rental assistant” (AI rental assistant). Describe your budget, whether it is per week or another period, whether it applies to a whole property, room or person, your household size, required bedrooms, bathrooms and parking, preferred areas and commute. You can start in English or Chinese. Avoid treating a monthly or per-person budget as a whole-property weekly advertisement price without checking the basis.

Continue in the same conversation as you move from housing requirements to recommendations, contracts and aftercare. Refreshing the page restores your saved conversation. Use “New case” when you need a separate rental workspace.

The chat can show requirement proposals, listings, comparisons, area sources and links to the relevant work screens. Review a proposed change and use its confirmation action before relying on it as saved case information. A chat response alone does not confirm a property selection or save a signed agreement.

## Edit rental requirements and see all matches

Open “My rental needs” (My rental requirements) to review or edit the current conditions. Check the budget amount, period and pricing scope; rental mode; household size; bedroom, bathroom and parking minimums; area; commute mode and maximum one-way minutes. Lifestyle preferences are also available for area reference. Confirm changes through the displayed action. If a message says the conversation or requirements have changed, reload the latest requirements before confirming.

Use the result controls to adjust a particular filter and apply the change. In “Housing recommendations” (Property recommendations), edited filter fields need “Apply filters” (Apply filters) before the displayed results reflect them. The results show their match count. All matching properties remain available through the result list, its expansion or pagination controls; the first visible cards are not the entire matching set. In chat, “View all …listings” expands a listing group; commute results have “All” (All) and area buttons plus controls to see more results.

When no property matches, review the offered changes to budget, room requirements, area or commute time. Applying a suggested relaxation changes the stated condition; read the change summary and remaining filters. You can also state the revised requirement in chat. Changing a lifestyle preference does not itself relax a hard housing or commute filter.

Open “Listing details” (Property details) for photos where available, advertised rent, room counts, commute information and a link to the original advertisement. An open-plan studio shown as a sleeping space is not proof of a separate bedroom. Verify availability, actual rent, what is included and the proposed tenancy directly with the advertiser.

## Commute estimates

Commute filtering is optional. If you have chosen a suburb such as Burwood, basic housing intake can continue without asking for a commute time or mode. If you are open to any suburb, after the basic housing details the assistant offers “Add commute filter” and “Skip commute filter”. These buttons prepare a reply in the chat box; send it to confirm your choice. Skipping keeps the search free of commute restrictions. Saving the revised rental needs confirms any removal of a previously saved commute condition. You can add a commute filter later in chat or “My rental needs”.

Current rental discovery focuses on the University of Sydney Camperdown / Darlington main campus, with Fisher Library used as the displayed reference point. Choose a supported mode such as public transport, walking or cycling and a maximum one-way duration. Discovery uses no fixed arrival time; it does not require you to invent a class start time to compare properties.

Choose “Public transport (any combination)” for transit, or Driving, Cycling or Walking. Public transport may use one transit mode or several: for example, walking to a train, transferring to a bus, then walking to campus. Older saved train, metro or bus preferences mean that the journey includes that mode; they do not require an exclusive single-mode trip.

Property details show Public transport, Driving, Cycling and eligible Walking references as compact cards in one grid. Each card shows an approximate duration or estimated range and a Google Maps link for that travel mode. The public transport card retains the valid recorded route selected for the current mode and transfer limit. Use Google Maps to check current services, stops and transfers. Detailed itinerary and segment panels are no longer part of this screen. The catalogue's “Commute to USYD” figure is a comparison reference based on available travel options. Check the individual mode cards in property details and confirm your journey in Google Maps. Filtering continues to use the selected travel mode, duration ceiling and transfer limits.

When only a neighbourhood estimate is available, the page shows its estimated range without inventing services, stops or transfers. A maximum commute requirement is checked against the upper bound of that range. Public transport directions in Google Maps can show different current services from the recorded reference.

Read the displayed approximate duration or estimated range for your selected mode. A range is a planning reference, not a guaranteed daily journey. Transport conditions and available route evidence can differ across properties. When commute information is missing, the card says “Check route” rather than proving the property meets your limit.

In the property details, “Google Maps” opens directions for the selected mode so you can check the journey independently. TfNSW and OpenStreetMap / FOSSGIS source references appear with the commute details. For a trip you actually intend to take, check the current route, stops and service information before travelling.

## Lifestyle preferences and sources

Tell the assistant whether parks, food and shopping, libraries or other supported lifestyle topics matter to you. “Find an area that fits your lifestyle” (Find your local area) shows relevant area context, source categories and coverage. Open the evidence details to inspect the supporting source and dates. Source categories distinguish official facility information, venue descriptions, media background and resident opinions.

Where sourced lifestyle ordering is available, it orders the properties that already meet the housing filters by coverage of your requested supported topics. Equal coverage preserves the earlier match order. It does not remove other matches, change your hard limits or establish that a property is suitable for you.

An area-level facility source does not establish distance from an individual home. Missing area coverage is not evidence that a facility is absent. Stale references, broader or neighbouring geography and background opinions do not establish a current local match. Quietness and safety are not established by these sources and do not contribute to the supported preference score. Check commute evidence separately.

## Favorites, comparison and direct selection

You have two parallel choices: select a single property directly when you are ready, or save several favorites and compare them first. Favoriting is optional; comparison is optional.

- Choose “Save listing” (Favorite property) on a card to save it. “Remove favorite” removes the favorite.
- Open “My favorites” (My favorites) to filter saved properties by address or area and open their details.
- Select 2–4 favorites to compare advertised weekly rent, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking and the displayed commute reference side by side. Chat also supports comparisons of the properties you select there.
- Choose “Select and follow up” (Select and follow up) on the property you want to carry forward. You can do this directly from a result without first favoriting or comparing it.

A favorite is not the current follow-up selection. The “Current follow-up property” label identifies the current selected property. A saved favorite that is no longer in the current catalogue may remain visible while selection is unavailable.

## Confirm selection and prepare an inspection

“Select this property and start follow-up?” (Select this property and start follow-up?) opens a confirmation dialog. Check the current property, advertised weekly rent and carried-over search conditions. Supply only the missing information requested, then choose “Confirm and start follow-up”. If another property is already selected, the dialog identifies the replacement; favorites are retained.

Confirmed selection records the property to follow up and opens the next steps. It does not book an inspection, submit a rental application or sign a tenancy. If the requirements change and the selection is marked historical, select again under the current requirements before continuing the contract workflow.

Open “Contact property agent” for the selected property. Use “Open listing & contact property agent” to visit the original advertisement and arrange an inspection with the advertiser. The app does not submit a booking.

In “Prepare an enquiry”, optionally enter the agent name, your preferred viewing time and other questions, then choose “Prepare enquiry”. This uses a local template and requires no AI API. Edit the subject and message, then use “Copy message”. Enter one agent email address to enable “Open email app”; check and send the draft yourself. The app does not send the email. Draft text stays while this screen is mounted, but is not saved after a refresh or when you change the selected property. No personal profile is required.

Expand “Inspection checklist” to read or download the questions. It helps you ask about:

- Whether the property is still available and when an inspection is possible.
- Actual rent, payment period and whether pricing is for the whole property, room or person.
- Included bills and other charges.
- Proposed start date, term and bond.
- Which bedrooms, bathrooms and parking spaces are included.
- Application documents, the proposed contract and landlord or agent details.

Contact the advertiser yourself and record the verified contract information in the contract screen. This checklist is a preparation aid; the screen does not provide an inspection booking or an agent-reply form. In chat, the selected property address and “Contact property agent” appear beside “Current housing criteria”. Close the contact drawer and use “Contract assistant” in the sidebar, then choose “Generate lease draft” or “Upload contract for review”.

## Lease draft form, AI assistance and PDF preview

In “Contract assistant” (Contract assistant), the lease draft workflow is for the supported NSW ordinary whole-property residential tenancy form. Check that it fits the proposed arrangement before confirming. The selected property's known details can provide defaults; verify each default rather than treating it as agreed tenancy information.

Use “Enter contract details directly” (Fill contract information directly) to enter the property, landlord, agent and tenant details; proposed dates and term; rent and bond; payment information; notice addresses; occupancy and other applicable form fields. Property and each party's notice address have separate fields. Optional signing information and emergency repair contacts can be entered where the form offers them.

You can save an incomplete draft; missing items stay blank or are listed as needing confirmation. Before saving, review the confirmation summary and acknowledge that this is proposed information for an unsigned draft. “Confirm and save unsigned draft” saves a version for later review.

“AI-assisted entry (optional)” (Optional AI-assisted filling) can extract draft fields from your description when AI is configured. It asks for consent to send that text to the configured external AI. Review the proposed fields before applying them to the form. Applying AI suggestions fills the form; saving the draft still requires a separate confirmation. Direct manual entry remains available.

After saving, use “PreviewSaved… contract” to preview that saved version in the official form, or download its PDF. The preview and download show the saved version even if you have unsaved form edits. Standard terms remain in the template and signature fields remain blank. Check the completed content, missing items and attachments with the other party and arrange manual signatures. Generating a draft is not a legal-validity guarantee and does not supply the other party's agreement or signature.

## Upload a contract for review

Open “Contract assistant” and choose “Upload contract for review”. Select a PDF, DOCX or TXT file up to 2 MB. Confirm that you are authorised to upload it and agree to send it to the AI service, then choose “Upload and analyse”. No property selection is required. The file is read first, then AI checks its details against the original text.

“Contract check results” shows the actual number of checks, how many need attention and how many passed. “Needs attention” lists identified issues and details that could not be confirmed, with an explanation and a suggested question where available. “Passed” lists details found in the contract and supported consistency checks. Use “View source text” on an item to see its original wording. Counts reflect the checks actually performed; missing details, unreadable text and incomplete AI analysis are not counted as passed.

If no issues are found, the result says that no issues were found in the completed checks. This does not establish the legal validity of every clause. Special clauses without a supported check remain items to confirm. If AI analysis does not complete, the document checks remain readable and you can consent to try AI again. The simplified result page does not require a human reviewer before you can read the findings.

“Upload another contract” starts a new review. “Stop processing this contract” withdraws permission for subsequent processing while keeping existing review records readable. AI results are a source-backed preview: they do not automatically change your saved contract details, selected property, tenancy or original document.

## Private contract search and separate consent

Private contract Q&A remains available separately in “AI rental assistant”. Ask to review or query your uploaded contract; if private search is not enabled, the conversation shows the contract search controls. Contract upload and AI review permission do not automatically enable private search.

- Keep the originating contract-processing permission active. Check “I agree to enable private search for this contract on this device.” and choose “Enable contract search”.
- To allow cloud search and answers, separately check “I also allow the AI service to process this contract for search and answers.” Leave it unchecked for local search.
- Enter a question and choose “Find contract text” for source excerpts. Per-question AI permission remains a separate choice.
- Use “Search permissions” and “Turn off contract search” to withdraw private search permission and remove the private index.

Private wording is kept separate from the public rental knowledge base. Check the cited contract text and use separately cited official guidance for legal questions.

## My home and in-progress items

In “Aftercare services”, use “My home” beside “Load latest information” to view the address, suburb, postcode and agent or landlord's name, email and phone. Existing saved home details take priority. If no location is saved, the selected property supplies the address, suburb and postcode; a confirmed lease source or current saved lease draft can supply a fallback. Draft contacts are shown only when they match the displayed home. Missing details remain “Not provided”. Opening the drawer is read-only and does not confirm a tenancy.

Use “Edit details” and “Save details” to explicitly save these six basic fields. Other saved profile information, files and service records are retained. Cancel editing leaves saved information unchanged. If another window changes the record, “Load latest details” refreshes untouched fields while preserving your local edits for another explicit save.

My home also displays saved electricity, gas, internet and water providers under “Utilities”. “Moving and cleaning” shows saved companies and service records, including their status, recorded dates, quotes and original notes. Completed services remain visible. “Change utilities” returns to provider selection, “Choose moving or cleaning” opens the company list, and “View record” opens the corresponding saved service record. These sections show saved information, not unsaved dropdown choices.

“In progress” collects unresolved repairs and service records that have not been marked completed. A past service date does not automatically complete a booking. Open an item to go to its repair or service record. Missing contact details are not added as tasks. The previous To-do items, Lease and living details and Home and contacts panels are replaced by these two toolbar buttons.

## Repair emails and follow-up records

In chat, describe the issue and requested email language and tone. The assistant shows the subject and full natural-language body before saving. Use “Copy email draft” to copy it immediately, or request a language or tone change. “Save to Aftercare” saves the exact preview after your confirmation; a missing recipient address does not block drafting. Historical previews remain copyable; rewrite controls belong to the latest assistant draft to avoid changing a different issue.

Open “Aftercare services” (Aftercare services), then “Repairs” (Day-to-day repairs). Review your saved home and contact information. Enter the landlord or agent name if known, a problem description, location, priority, email language and tone. You can prepare a draft directly or use “Polish email with AI” (AI polish email).

Clicking the polish action sends the form's original text and greeting information to the configured AI to produce an editable subject and body. Read and edit the result, then confirm saving the repair draft. If you change the original form after polishing, check the warning and regenerate or review the saved wording before continuing.

Saving or polishing does not send the email. Use “Copy email draft” (Copy email draft) or, when an email address is available, “Open in email app” (Open in email client). You review the recipient and send it yourself in your own email application. Without an address you can still save and copy the draft.

Update the saved repair status and use “Record repair follow-up” (Record repair follow-up) to record a reply or your own notes. “Adjust email tone” changes draft wording for the existing repair. These actions update the repair record without contacting the recipient. For an urgent problem, use the saved emergency contact and the official guidance shown in the interface rather than relying on a draft as an emergency response.

## Utilities, moving and cleaning providers

In “Utilities” (Utilities), open provider cards to view the provider's coverage, contact details, official website, source and checked date. Where available, details include plan or tariff information and its source. Confirm current availability, price and conditions for your actual address with the provider.

Select electricity, gas, internet and the applicable water provider in the form, then use “Save provider selection” (Save provider choices) and confirm. This stores your choices in the workspace; it does not open or switch an account. The water information is shown for the provider serving the address rather than as an unrestricted switching choice.

After saving, choose “View in My home” to see all saved utility providers together with your home information. The same selections are restored after a page reload.

In “Moving and cleaning” (Moving and cleaning), click a company card to open its details and official website. “Record quote or booking” (Record a quote or booking) lets you save a quote you received, a service date, status and notes. Status choices distinguish considering, contacted by you, booked by you and completed.

To save a company before obtaining a quote, use “Save to My home” in its details and confirm. This creates a “Considering” service record with no invented date or quote. A company that already has a saved record offers “View in My home” instead of another quick-save. “Record quote or booking” remains available for additional records. The “View in My home” button on the company list opens all saved moving and cleaning records.

Saving a service record does not submit a booking. Contact the company and arrange the work yourself; provider availability, service coverage and the quote need the company's confirmation. Return to “My service records” to update the record after your own arrangements.

## Issue report and history

In “Aftercare services”, open “Issue report”. This page has two tabs: “Report issue” and “History”. Older Move-out and bond links open the same simplified page.

Under “Report issue”, choose the issue type: rent/bond, furniture, repairs, an issue during the tenancy, moving out, after moving out or other. Enter the original issue description and optionally use “Upload files” to add photos, screenshots or PDF evidence. Up to 10 files can be attached, with a maximum of 10 MB each. Choose “Submit report” to save and send it to Human review. The submission date is recorded automatically by the server. No lease setup, confirmed tenancy, move-out plan or bond activation is required to submit an issue.

“History” shows previous reports, their original descriptions, automatic dates, uploaded files, status and human review advice. It includes reports submitted through the older move-out screen. Saved amounts, requested help and supporting references for those older reports remain available under “Original report details”. Files can be previewed or downloaded. “Refresh history” reloads the latest reports and advice. Failed submissions preserve the description and selected files; “Retry original action” reuses the original request rather than creating a duplicate report.

The Human review page loads the queue directly without a key or a selected tenant case. Each report shows the issue, reported details and evidence. Reviewers can inspect images and documents, enter “Review advice” and select “Save advice and complete review”. The saved advice appears in the user's History. Completed items remain available in Human review under “History”. If a report version changes while advice is being written, “Load latest report” preserves that advice and loads the new information before an explicit save.

Reports and files stay in local storage; this reporting flow does not send evidence to external AI. Completing human review does not record a refund, accept a deduction or submit an external dispute. Original reports and earlier records are retained.

## Read sources and handle changes

Ask general rental questions or questions about using the product directly in “AI rental assistant”. For example, ask “How do I request repairs?” Relevant public documents are retrieved automatically, and the answer and citations appear in the conversation. Open “Read source excerpts” to inspect the supporting passages and check any stated uncertainty. You can ask a knowledge question while recording housing requirements and then continue the same conversation. Public product documentation explains controls; official guidance supplies tenancy information.

Public knowledge is separate from uploaded private contracts. To ask about a particular contract, use the private contract controls in chat and enable the relevant document search and AI processing permissions. A general rental question does not enable access to a private contract.

Saved data and current form input can differ. If you see a stale-version warning or a failed-save message, use the offered reload or retry action, compare the latest information with your retained input and confirm again. A visible proposed value or an unsaved preview is not proof that a write completed. Return to the relevant feature page to view its saved results.
